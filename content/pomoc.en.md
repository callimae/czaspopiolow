---
title: "Help in a crisis"
description: "Free crisis helplines in Poland, and where to find one in your country."
---

If you are in crisis right now - don't read the blog. Call. It's not weakness; it's the most sensible thing a person can do.

This blog is written from Poland, so the numbers below are Polish:

**Life-threatening emergency - [112](tel:112)**

Yours or anyone's. Don't hesitate.

**Support Centre for people in mental crisis - [800 70 2222](tel:800702222)**

Free, 24/7. Psychologists answer. You can also write: [centrumwsparcia.pl](https://centrumwsparcia.pl).

**Crisis helpline - [116 123](tel:116123)**

For adults in emotional crisis. Free.

**Helpline for children and teenagers - [116 111](tel:116111)**

Free, 24/7.

---

If you are outside Poland, [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) lists free, verified helplines by country.

Beyond the phone: in Poland you can walk into a psychiatric hospital's admission room **without a referral**, at any hour. And if it's not a right-now crisis but something that lasts - a GP or a psychologist is a good first step; you don't need a referral to see a psychiatrist in Poland.
